The line we refused to cross

The old compromise was never necessary.

Browser agents usually ask for one of two concessions: expose credentials, or let sensitive page contents follow the agent. Pratik and Muse chose a different architecture—live observation, explicit consent and authority that stays on the user’s machine.

Credentials stay local.Passwords never leave your machine. Sign-in becomes a human takeover.
Context stays live.Observe what the task needs instead of retaining a private-page archive.
Consent is explicit.Every prompt offers once, site, hour, day or deny.
Authority fails closed.Dismissal means no action. T3 always asks.

Consent is not a setting. It is a moment.

Stage the action, then choose its exact scope. The route does not complete until you decide. This demo keeps no state when you leave.

Two liabilities removed at the architecture.

01 / Credential exposure

The password never enters the conversation.

Muse drives the authenticated browser session you already own. When sign-in is needed, control returns to you on your machine. Passwords and one-time codes never need to touch the agent or relay.

02 / Sensitive-data memory

Live observation replaces retention.

The agent observes what is needed for the current task, acts with explicit permission, then moves on. Private page contents do not need to become retained personal history.

Session grants / shipped in 0.1.9

Consent that protects the flow.

For multi-step work, choose 1 hour or 1 day. T1 and T2 actions can continue inside that window. T3 sensitive actions still stop and ask—every time.
